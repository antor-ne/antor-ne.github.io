Career Summary

M.Sc. researcher in nuclear engineering, working on reactor physics, neutronics, and computational modeling. Current focus is building OpenMC-based models of the BAEC TRIGA Research Reactor, alongside GEANT4 particle-transport work and physics-informed machine learning applied to reactor behavior. Published research spans fuel neutronics, MOX-loaded cores, tritium breeding, and advanced fuel assemblies. Also active in student leadership and community organizing at the University of Dhaka.
